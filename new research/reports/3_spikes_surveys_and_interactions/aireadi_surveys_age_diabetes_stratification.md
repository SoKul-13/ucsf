### 💡 Key Findings Across Survey Partitions
1. **Age & Diabetes Interaction**: Older Adults ($>65$) with Insulin-Dependent or Oral-Controlled Diabetes exhibit the highest rates of cognitive impairment ($\text{MoCA} < 26$) and the highest PAID-5 diabetes distress scores.
2. **Educational Buffer**: Education level remains consistent across age groups, reinforcing its role as an independent covariate in cognitive modeling.
